User guidelines
What steady is for

steady is a daily ledger for gradual, steady body recomposition — not a crash diet.

  • Log meals by text or photo and get AI-estimated macros
  • Calorie and macro targets that adjust with your goal and today's activity
  • Short reflections and a 1–10 score from Aino, your AI-nutritionist
  • 4-week trends and body measurements
  • Save recipes as favorites for quick-tap logging
  • Export or delete your data any time
Who is Aino

Aino is steady's AI-nutritionist — not a real person, and not a licensed dietitian. She's Claude, given a nutrition-focused persona: warm and concise, and grounded fresh in your actual logged data every time she responds, rather than working from a fixed memory of you.

Her guidance follows one consistent stance: gradual, steady progress, never extreme restriction or rapid weight loss. She won't suggest skipping meals or crash-dieting, however a question is phrased.

Her estimates and reflections are AI-generated and can be wrong — review them the way you would any estimate, and talk to a doctor or registered dietitian for medical or personalized nutrition advice.

How Aino works

Aino shows up in three places:

  • A short tip on an individual meal
  • A one-time reflection for a completed day (“This day summary”) or the trailing 7 days (“Last 7 days summary”)
  • A live back-and-forth chat for today only — once the date changes, that conversation isn't shown again; you'll see a summary of the day instead, not the chat itself

Past days and the trailing week get a single regeneratable summary rather than an ongoing conversation.

AI features draw on your own Claude usage, not a separate cost. Normal day-to-day use — a few meals, occasional tips or short chats — is a small draw on your plan's allowance. Long back-and-forth conversations with Aino in one sitting use meaningfully more, since the whole conversation resends each time you send a message.

How targets work

Your calorie and macro targets are calculated fresh every time, from:

  • Your goal — recomposition, fat loss, muscle gain, or maintenance
  • Your training frequency
  • Whatever activity you've logged today — a workout adds calories back for that day only

Nothing is cached, so changing your goal or activity updates your targets instantly.

The 1–10 score
Meal tips and both summaries include a 1–10 score for how well that meal, day, or week supports your goal — not a moral judgment of the food. A day's score isn't an average of its meals, and a week's score isn't an average of its days: Aino weighs the whole pattern rather than letting one weak spot drag down an otherwise solid stretch.
Best practices
  • AI estimates — calories, macros, tips — are a starting point, not a lab result. Review and edit them, especially for homemade meals or uncertain portions.
  • Adding ingredients and amounts to a meal noticeably improves the quality of tips and summaries, since Aino can see what's actually in a meal, not just its macros.
  • Demo mode writes real data to your storage the same as normal use — editing or getting a tip on a demo day saves it for real.
  • Using steady on more than one device close together: the most recent save wins silently, with no conflict warning.
  • “Today” follows your browser's local clock, so logging right around midnight or while traveling can land a meal on a date you didn't expect.
Troubleshooting
  • If you see “Nothing you enter is being saved right now,” it usually means the artifact isn't published — publish it and reload, or try a hard refresh if it's already published. If that doesn't help, it may be a temporary issue with the storage service itself, not something wrong with your data.
  • Photo logging may not work inside the Claude mobile app's built-in view — if “Choose file” does nothing when tapped, try opening this artifact's link in your phone's regular browser instead, or use text logging, which isn't affected.
Data & privacy
  • Requires Claude Pro or higher. Saving your meals, profile, and history only works if you're on a Pro, Max, Team, or Enterprise plan. On the Free plan, you can open and try the app, but nothing you enter will be saved between visits.
  • Your data is private to you. Everything is stored under your own Claude account — not visible to the person who built this app, and not visible to anyone else using this same published tool.
  • Where it lives. Your entries are stored in Anthropic's storage system for this artifact, tied to your Claude account — not in your browser, and not on any server the developer controls. The developer does not operate or maintain a separate database of any kind — there's no copy of your data outside Anthropic's platform. As with all data on the Claude platform, how it's collected, used, and retained is governed by Anthropic's Privacy Policy and Consumer Terms of Service — not a separate policy set by this app.
  • AI features use your own usage, not a separate cost. Meal estimation and Aino, the AI-nutritionist chat, draw from your existing Claude subscription — no extra billing.
  • Photos aren't kept. A meal photo is analyzed once for its estimate, then discarded — the resulting numbers and a short AI-written description (name and ingredients) are saved, not the photo itself. Worth keeping the frame to just your food, since anything else in the shot is briefly sent along for that one-time analysis too.
